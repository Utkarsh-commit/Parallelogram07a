Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Parallelogram ("we," "us," "our") collects when you use this Site, why we collect it, and how it's handled. We keep this simple on purpose — we collect the minimum needed to deliver your guides and run the business, nothing more.

1.What We Collect

  • Name and email address — collected when you claim the free guide, make a purchase, or contact us. This is how we deliver guides and communicate about your order.
  • Order details — which guide(s) you purchased/claimed and when, so we can resend a lost guide or answer support questions without you needing an account.
  • Search queries — if you use the on-site search, your search text is logged (without your name/email attached) so we can see which topics people are looking for and improve the guide catalog.
  • Coupon code usage — which discount codes get used and how often, to understand what's effective.
  • Payment information — we do not collect or store your card details. Payments are handled entirely by Lemon Squeezy, PayPal, or your chosen payment provider, under their own privacy policies.

We do not collect sensitive personal data (health, religion, political views, etc.), and we don't want to — please don't send us any in messages.

2.Why We Collect It

  • To deliver the guide(s) you paid for or claimed for free
  • To let you recover a lost guide by re-entering your email — no password needed
  • To respond to support questions
  • To understand what's popular (searches, guide popularity) so we can improve the Site and catalog
  • To detect and prevent abuse (e.g., someone trying to exploit the free guide form repeatedly)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, ever.

3.Third-Party Services We Use

To run the Site, we rely on a small number of trusted service providers, each of which processes limited data on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

4.How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep order records (email + what you purchased) so the "resend my guide" feature works whenever you need it — indefinitely, unless you ask us to delete it (see your rights below). Search logs are kept in aggregate form and are not tied to your identity.

5.Cookies & Local Storage

We use minimal local browser storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to remember things like your cart contents during a session, so you don't lose your selections while browsing. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

6.Your Rights

Regardless of where you're located, you can ask us at any time to:

  • See what personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your data (note: this will also remove your ability to use the "resend guide" feature, since it relies on that record existing)

To exercise any of these, just email us — no formal process needed.

📧 For any privacy request or question, email parallelogramguides@gmail.com. We'll get back to you personally — this isn't a big company with a support ticket queue.

7.Children's Privacy

Our Site and guides are intended for a general adult audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8.Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change meaningfully, we'll update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.

9.Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach out at parallelogramguides@gmail.com.
